    About the numbersEvery worked example is a composite illustration, built so the arithmetic holds together and can be checked — not a case history of a real, named company. Confidence intervals, sample sizes, chi-squared values and revenue figures were computed rather than estimated, so you are welcome to redo any of them. Where a figure is an assumption, it says so.

    How to use itNot professional advice. It is a teaching reference: the processes are opinionated, the thresholds are conventions rather than laws, and every judgement here should lose to evidence from your own product. Corrections and disagreements are genuinely welcome.
